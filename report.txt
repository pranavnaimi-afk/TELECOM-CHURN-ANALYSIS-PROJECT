### Data Analytics Project Report

**Domain:** Telecommunications

---

## 1. Introduction

Customer churn refers to the number of customers who stop using a company's products or services within a specific period.

In the telecommunications industry, retaining existing customers is important for business growth and profitability.

This project focuses on analyzing telecom customer data to identify the major factors influencing customer churn and provide business recommendations to reduce customer loss.

## 2. Problem Statement

The telecom company is experiencing customer loss. The company wants to understand why customers are leaving and identify customer groups that have a higher possibility of leaving.

The main objective is to analyze customer information, service usage, contract details, and monthly charges to identify churn patterns.

## 3. Project Objectives

- Analyze customer churn patterns.
- Identify customer groups with higher churn rates.
- Understand the impact of internet services on churn.
- Analyze the relationship between monthly charges and churn.
- Identify the impact of payment methods and billing preferences.
- Provide business recommendations to improve customer retention.

## 4. Dataset Description

The dataset contains customer information and service-related details.

Important columns include:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| customerID | Unique customer identification |
| gender | Customer gender |
| SeniorCitizen | Senior citizen status |
| Partner | Whether customer has a partner |
| Dependents | Whether customer has dependents |
| tenure | Number of months with company |
| InternetService | Type of internet service |
| OnlineSecurity | Online security subscription |
| TechSupport | Technical support subscription |
| Contract | Contract type |
| PaperlessBilling | Paperless billing status |
| PaymentMethod | Customer payment method |
| MonthlyCharges | Monthly service charges |
| TotalCharges | Total amount charged |
| Churn | Whether customer left the company |

## 6. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

EDA was performed to understand customer behavior and identify patterns associated with customer churn.

### 6.1 Overall Customer Churn

The churn distribution was analyzed to understand how many customers stayed with the company and how many customers left.

**Business Insight:** The company should focus on understanding the reasons behind customer loss and develop strategies to improve customer retention.

### 6.2 Internet Service Analysis

The analysis compares churn across different internet service categories, including Fiber Optic, DSL, and No Internet.

**Observation:** Fiber Optic customers appear to have a higher churn rate in the analyzed graph.

**Business Recommendation:**
- Improve internet speed and connection stability.
- Provide better technical support.
- Offer suitable customer retention packages.
- Collect feedback from Fiber Optic customers.

### 6.3 Paperless Billing Analysis

The relationship between paperless billing and customer churn was analyzed.

**Observation:** Paperless billing customers show a higher churn level in the analyzed graph.

**Business Recommendation:**
- Improve the online billing experience.
- Provide clear billing statements.
- Send payment reminders and notifications.
- Resolve billing-related complaints quickly.

### 6.4 Monthly Charges Analysis

Monthly charges were analyzed to identify whether customers paying higher amounts are more likely to leave.

**Observation:** Customers with monthly charges above $80 appear to be an important group to investigate for higher churn.

**Business Recommendation:**
- Provide special discounts.
- Introduce affordable monthly packages.
- Offer additional benefits.
- Provide loyalty rewards for long-term customers.

### 6.5 Contract Analysis

Customer churn was compared across month-to-month, one-year, and two-year contracts.

**Business Recommendation:** Encourage customers to consider longer-term plans by providing suitable benefits and competitive pricing.

### 6.6 Technical Support Analysis

The relationship between technical support subscriptions and customer churn was examined.

**Business Recommendation:** Improve customer support services and make technical assistance easily accessible.

## 7. Key Business Insights

Based on the EDA observations, the following areas require attention:

1. **Fiber Optic:** Improve internet quality and technical support.
2. **Paperless Billing:** Improve the digital billing experience.
3. **High Monthly Charges:** Provide suitable discounts and affordable plans.
4. **Contract Type:** Encourage long-term customer relationships.
5. **Technical Support:** Resolve customer complaints quickly.

*Note: These are example findings based on the observed patterns. Actual churn percentages should be calculated before confirming each conclusion.*

## 8. Business Recommendations

The telecom company can reduce customer churn through the following strategies:

- Introduce customer-specific retention offers.
- Improve internet service quality.
- Provide better customer support.
- Offer affordable subscription plans.
- Improve digital payment and billing services.
- Collect customer feedback regularly.
- Identify customers with a high risk of leaving and contact them proactively.

## 9. Conclusion

The Telecom Customer Churn Analysis project helps identify customer behavior and factors associated with customer loss.

Exploratory Data Analysis provides useful insights into internet services, billing preferences, monthly charges, and contract types.

By improving service quality, providing suitable offers, and understanding customer requirements, the telecom company can improve customer satisfaction and reduce customer churn.
